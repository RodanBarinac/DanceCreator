Token Rotation Test

Single shape definition: a square with arrowhead pointing right. Positioned locally at origin (0,0), then translated to different locations with rotation applied. The entire group rotates together.

Key concept: The token shape (square + arrow) is defined once at the origin with the arrow pointing right. Each instance uses <use href="#token"/> and then translate() and rotate() are applied to position and rotate the entire group together. The arrow always rotates with the square, demonstrating proper facing direction indication.
